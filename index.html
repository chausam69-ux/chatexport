FAQ

How do I export a WhatsApp chat?

Android: open the chat → ⋮ → More → Export chat. iPhone: open the chat → tap the name → Export Chat. Save the file to your phone or send it to yourself, then open it here.

Can I convert a WhatsApp chat to PDF?

Yes. Load the chat, apply any filters, click Export PDF. Your browser’s print dialog opens — choose “Save as PDF”.

Do photos show up?

If you exported “with media” as a .zip, photos appear inline next to their messages. Videos and audio are listed by file name.

Is this really private?

Yes. Everything runs in your browser. You can verify with the network tab in developer tools — no requests are made after the page loads.

Which formats are supported?

WhatsApp .txt exports from Android and iOS (12h and 24h clocks, any date order), and .zip exports with media.
