FAQ

Is the PDF accepted as evidence?

Courts generally accept printed chat exports alongside the original file; the PDF keeps timestamps and sender names exactly as WhatsApp wrote them. Keep the original .txt too.

Do photos appear in the PDF?

Yes if you exported with media (.zip). Photos are placed inline next to their messages.

Does it work on iPhone exports?

Yes. Both Android and iOS export formats, 12-hour and 24-hour clocks.
